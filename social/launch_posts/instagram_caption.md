### Reel Caption Copy

What if an entire civilization only vanishes when the last living person stops remembering it? 🌌✨

For #TripothonS1, our team built "A Gift for the Forgotten City"—an interactive 3D web experience where you explore a fractured, monochrome void and restore four erased cultures back to reality. 

Instead of fighting monsters or grinding for gear, your only weapon is the act of remembrance. Finding ancient cultural Echoes triggers real-time shader transitions that flood the world with bioluminescent light, orchestral music, and architectural splendor—culminating in an unlockable fifth sanctuary where all four civilizations unite.

🛠️ The Tech Behind the Magic:
• 🤖 3D Generation: All 25+ buildings, relics, and characters were generated using the @tripo3d V3 API (Text-to-3D ➔ Quad Retopology ➔ PBR Rebake ➔ Auto-Rigging).
• 🌐 Engine: Pure Three.js (r160) running at a locked 60 FPS directly in your mobile and desktop browser. Zero downloads or installations required!
• 🥽 Virtual Reality: Full WebXR 6DoF support for PICO 4 and PICO Neo 3 headsets—just open the browser and tap "Enter VR".
• 🎵 Generative Audio: Dynamic interactive soundscapes synthesized via Web Audio API.
