### Video Description Body

Can generative AI create an entire cohesive 3D game universe in just five days? 

Welcome to Dev Diary #1 of "A Gift for the Forgotten City", our official submission for Tripothon S1! In this video, we pull back the curtain on how we built an atmospheric 3D web exploration and cultural restoration game natively in Three.js and WebXR, powered by the Tripo V3 API.

You play as "The Rememberer", wandering through a silent, desaturated void where four ancient civilizations have slipped from memory. By seeking out scattered cultural Echoes, you trigger real-time shader transitions that bring color, architecture, and procedural music roaring back into the world, unlocking the radiant central sanctuary: The Grand Reunion.

We cover everything from prompt engineering for 3D generation and automated quad retopology, to Web Audio synthesizers and getting 60 FPS WebXR running on the PICO 4.

### 🛠️ Tech Stack & Tools Mentioned
- Tripo AI (V3 OpenAPI): Text-to-3D, AI Quad Retopology (5k faces), PBR Texture Rebaking, Auto-Rigging & Animation
- Three.js (r160): Modern ESM WebGL rendering engine with EffectComposer post-processing
- WebXR Device API: Native virtual reality support for PICO 4 and PICO Neo 3
- Web Audio API: Procedural spatial soundscapes and dynamic filter transitions
- Pollinations.ai: Initial architectural concept art references
